Castle Adventure

Back to Home

A dungeon crawler — survive the castle and open all 4 chests to escape.

Goal

  1. Start in the first room and head south into the castle.
  2. Defeat all enemies in each room to unlock the chest.
  3. Walk into the chest to open it — this removes the red gates blocking the doors.
  4. Clear all rooms and open the final chest to win.

Controls

Arrow keys Move your character
Left click Shoot toward your cursor

Enemies

  • Walker

    Moves randomly, pauses, then picks a new direction.

    2 HP

  • Seeker

    Chases you directly across the room.

    3 HP

  • Shooter

    Stays still and fires bullets at you regularly.

    4 HP

  • Tank

    Slow, but deals heavy damage on contact.

    8 HP

Health

You have 10 HP shown in the top right corner. Each contact with an Enemy or an Enemy's bullet will hurt you. After each hit you get brief invincibility frames so you won't lose HP instantly.

Scoring

When you win, your remaining HP and completion time are recorded. Finish fast and take as little damage as possible for the best score.

Tip: The timer only starts once you enter the first room.
